# SMoRFFI — Dataset Qualification

## Decision
**KEEP — primary same-model dataset**

## Responsibility
D3–D6 and D10; D7/D8 only after package-level metadata verification.

## Evidence summary
The 2026 Computer Networks data article reports 123 same-model commercial IEEE 802.11g devices, 35.42 million raw I/Q samples and 1.85 million RF features, with a reproducible collection-to-evaluation framework.

## Strength
Its 123-device same-model population directly addresses the difficult problem of distinguishing physically similar devices without relying on obvious model-level differences.

## Limitations
Current evidence does not establish sufficient receiver/day/environment/sequential coverage for primary D7/D8 use. Those claims must not be inferred from dataset scale.

## Scientific boundary
KEEP is qualification for development use, not scientific validation.
